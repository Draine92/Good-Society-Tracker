# Setting and Reskinned Decks v1 (draft)

Companion to the hybrid rules. High fantasy, Regency-style politics: many noble houses, and families of wealth and means.
Card numbers (#1-66) match the base Good Society deck, so the playsets and the rules doc still point to the right slots. All card text below is original wording for this setting. Names, places and details are placeholders and meant to be changed.

---

## 1. Setting primer (seed)

**The Concord.** A realm of old Houses bound together by the Concord, a treaty-oath that sets rank, precedence and who may marry whom. The capital hosts **the Season**, when the Houses gather for balls, hunts and negotiations. The countryside is run from great estates, most of them tied to a source of magic (a ley-well, a bonded dragon, an ancestral ward-stone).

**Core tensions to play with**
- **Bound estates:** an estate and its magic pass by oath, not just by will. Inheritance disputes can be literally magical.
- **Marriage pacts:** many marriages are contracts that bind bloodlines and wards. Breaking one has consequences.
- **Standing:** the Houses judge everyone. Reputation decides whose wards open to you.
- **Magic as etiquette:** duels, charms and scrying on a guest are all serious breaches of manners, though some people do them anyway.
- **Old wealth vs. new wealth:** the Counting-Houses are buying their way into rooms that were closed to them.

**Family backgrounds, reskinned** (choose one as a starting package: two starting reputation tags and a social standing)
| Base | Fantasy equivalent | Flavour |
|---|---|---|
| Old Money | **Ancient House** | Centuries of land and tradition |
| Peerage | **High House** | Holds a seat and a title in the Concord |
| New Money | **Counting-House** | Trade and industry, rich but not welcome |
| Humble Origins | **Lesser Line** | Poor branch of a good name |
| Clergy | **Temple House** | Moral weight, modest purse |
| Military | **Banner House** | Sworn swords, honour and discipline |
| Ill-Reputed | **Shadowed House** | A scandal sits over every dinner |
| Foreign | **Far-Court** | From another realm, culture or plane |

Any species can appear, and the Concord does not treat species as a barrier to rank. It does treat origin as one.

## 2. Desire cards (#1-22)

Format: type, desire, description, and an adventure **Hook** so the desire also pulls the party into D&D play.
"+Tag" means start with one extra negative reputation tag. "+Insp" means start with 1 extra Inspiration. Rules from the base deck are kept: mandatory connections, public knowledge, set-up tasks.

**#01. Basic. Redeem a disgrace.**
Win back a former friend's forgiveness and your good name. One rash act cost you both. Create and spread a rumour about what you did. Start with +Tag and +Insp.
*Hook:* The friend is in danger, and only you can help.

**#02. Complex, Age. Be acknowledged.**
Make a powerful parent recognise you publicly as their child and heir to the House. They refused you from the cradle. You will use charm, favours or worse.
*Hook:* Prove your lineage through a trial, a relic or a long-lost witness.

**#03. Basic. Keep your options open.**
Receive or make at least two successful proposals from eligible suitors. Marry one and quietly release the other.
*Hook:* A suitor's family has dungeons, debts or enemies attached.

**#04. Complex. Take revenge on your rival.**
They took what you loved. Work with their player to decide what it was. You have waited a long time for this.
*Hook:* The stolen thing is an object or place you can go and take back.

**#05. Basic. Solve a debt secretly.**
Come into substantial wealth fast, while keeping your reputation spotless. Your debts are severe and your creditors are patient but not kind.
*Hook:* A treasure or contract could settle the debt, if you can reach it.

**#06. Basic. Take your sibling's seat.**
Convince your House to disinherit your elder sibling and name you heir. You need one scandal or one mistake. Create a parent as a mandatory connection.
*Hook:* Find the proof, or manufacture the incident.

**#07. Basic. Play matchmaker.**
Orchestrate at least one marriage between people of your choosing. After the first session, name two potential couples and tell the DM. Succeed with one or both.
*Hook:* A couple's families are feuding, or the match needs a rare gift or blessing.

**#08. Complex. Restore the family name.**
Ten years ago your House broke an oath in a scandal that shook the realm, and fled. You are back to claim your place. Not everyone is pleased.
Public knowledge: the scandal. Create and spread two negative rumours about your House.
*Hook:* Make amends for the broken oath, or find out what really happened.

**#09. Basic. Earn consent.**
Raise your beloved's reputation, then win your parent's permission to marry. Only an impeccable name will do. Create a parent as a mandatory connection.
*Hook:* Your beloved's reputation needs a heroic deed or a clearing of old charges.

**#10. Basic. Be loved for yourself.**
You have just inherited a vast fortune: a hoard, a title or a magical estate. Marry someone who loves you without knowing about it.
*Hook:* The inheritance has strings: guardians, a curse, or claimants.

**#11. Basic. Guard your sibling.**
Your younger sibling is naive and vulnerable. Protect them from every ill and choose who they marry. After the first session, name the match you want.
*Hook:* A dangerous suitor, a kidnapper or a curse stalks them.

**#12. Complex. The double game.**
Appear to court the partner your wealthy aunt chose for you, while secretly becoming engaged to the one you love. She is wealthy, dying and watching. Create your aunt as a mandatory connection.
*Hook:* The aunt's estate has a guardian, a will-ward or a rival claimant.

**#13. Basic. Claim what's yours.**
You were disowned for something shocking three years ago. You are back to claim your inheritance. Create and spread a rumour about what you did. Start with +Tag and +Insp.
Public knowledge: you were disinherited. If you lack the parent relationship card, create a parent as a mandatory connection.
*Hook:* Win your House's trust with a deed or a returned relic.

**#14. Basic. Turn the tables.**
You lived in an enemy's shadow while they got every advantage. Now turn their connections against them.
*Hook:* Their allies have secrets you can find in places others avoid.

**#15. Complex. Break the engagement.**
Your reckless family ruined itself, and your father arranged your engagement to a rich stranger to avoid selling the estate. Fix the family's finances, then call it off.
Public knowledge: you are engaged.
*Hook:* Save the family with a quest, a sale, or a marriage for someone else.

**#16. Basic. Marry your secret betrothed.**
You and another were secretly engaged three years ago. Their feelings are fading, and the marriage is also your only route to a rank you could never reach.
*Hook:* Win them back through a grand gesture, or find out why they've cooled.

**#17. Basic. Outshine your cousin.**
Beat your perfect cousin in every contest and win your uncle's inheritance, the seat of the House and the magic that comes with it. Create your uncle as a mandatory connection.
*Hook:* The uncle sets trials, and your cousin is ahead.

**#18. Basic. Rekindle.**
Five years ago, you broke a secret engagement under pressure. Find out if they still love you, and re-establish the engagement.
*Hook:* Their circumstances have changed, perhaps into danger.

**#19. Complex. Divide them.**
The one you love has fallen for someone else. You have waited too long. Break up the pair by any means. If your relationship card is Rival, decide who your love is.
*Hook:* The paramour has a vulnerability, an enemy or a secret.

**#20. Basic. Impress your patron.**
Marry in a way your patron approves, to keep their support. You owe them your career. Show this card to your patron.
*Hook:* The patron's approval comes with a task.

**#21. Basic, Age. Marry your child well.**
Marry your child to someone extremely wealthy or of the High Houses. They are your best asset and you intend to use them.
*Hook:* The target match is protected, secret or unwilling.

**#22. Complex. End the feud.**
Two Houses have been at war since your grandparents' day, and it is costing both of them their futures. End it through marriage or inheritance.
Public knowledge: the feud, and who it is between.
*Hook:* The feud started over a lost relic, a death or a broken pact.

## 5. Notes and next steps
- Some desires are intentionally close to the base cards so existing playsets and the mapping in the rules doc still work. Change any freely.
- #2 and #21 need a player willing to play an older character. #19 and #12 are the most complicated, so I'd keep them for experienced players.
- Next candidates: a playset (which 3 desires and relationship ring suit your group), a map of the major Houses, a starter list of NPC Houses, and a sample session-zero questionnaire.
